**Effective date:** December 14, 2025

Thank you for playing Tessera: Classic Block Puzzle ("the game", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information about you when you play our game.

By using the game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

### a. Information You Provide to Us

*   **Contact Information:** If you contact us for support, we may collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.

### b. Information Collected Automatically

*   **Usage Data:** We collect information about how you play the game, such as your score, levels achieved, and other in-game interactions. This data is stored locally on your device.
*   **Device Information:** We collect information about the device you use to play the game, including the device model, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
*   **Analytics Data:** We use third-party services like Google Analytics for Firebase to collect and analyze usage data to help us improve the game. This may include your IP address and mobile network information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

*   **To Provide and Maintain the Game:** To operate and maintain the game, including saving your game progress locally on your device.
*   **To Improve and Personalize the Game:** To understand how our players use the game and to improve their experience.
*   **For Customer Support:** To respond to your questions and support requests.
*   **For Analytics:** To monitor and analyze trends and usage of the game.
*   **To Comply with Law:** To comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

*   **With Service Providers:** We may share information with third-party companies that provide services on our behalf, such as data analysis (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase).
*   **For Legal Reasons:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our players, or others.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Your game data, including your progress and scores, is stored locally on your device's database. We do not store this data on our servers.

**If you uninstall the game, all your game data will be permanently deleted from your device and cannot be recovered.**

## 5. Advertising

We may partner with third-party advertising networks to display advertisements within the game in the future. These partners may collect and use information about you, such as your device identifier, to provide you with personalized advertising. We will update this policy when advertising is implemented.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, since your data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data also depends on the security of your device.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our game is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
